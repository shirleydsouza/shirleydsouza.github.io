About

Helping engineers ship with confidence

Over six years at Salesforce I grew from intern to Lead Member of Technical Staff. My work sits where CI/CD, cloud infrastructure and reliability meet: I design services that record every build artifact on its way to production, require test evidence and compliance before a release moves forward, and roll changes out gradually behind automatic guardrails.

I like taking ambiguous, cross-team problems from first design through production rollout, and I care about mentoring, thoughtful code review and a team that wins together. Before Salesforce I spent three years building trade finance systems at JPMorgan Chase in Mumbai, then earned my master's in Computer Science at USC.

Release safety

Pre-deployment blocking checks, artifact invalidation, and release gates driven by integration-test evidence.

Progressive delivery

Canary selection, restrictive rollout strategies and fleet-wide rollout guardrails.

Cloud platform

Kubernetes on AWS EKS, event-driven services on SNS/SQS, and infrastructure as code with Terraform and Helm.
